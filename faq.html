Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about admissions, programs, fees, internships, careers and student life at Rwanda School of Hospitality.

Showing Admissions questions

Admissions

How can I apply to the school?
Applications can be started by contacting the admissions office or submitting an enquiry through the school's official admissions process. You should prepare the required identification and academic documents before submitting your application.
What documents are required for admission?
Required documents may include academic certificates, identification documents, passport photographs and other documents requested by the admissions office.
Can I contact admissions before applying?
Yes. Prospective students are encouraged to contact the admissions team with questions about programs, requirements, fees and the application process.

Programs & Courses

What programs does the school offer?
The school focuses on hospitality and tourism education, including areas such as Food & Beverage Service, Food & Beverage Operation and Tourism.
Are practical skills included in the courses?
Hospitality education combines academic knowledge with practical skills. Students can develop professional abilities through practical training and hospitality-focused learning activities.
Is the school focused only on hotel operations?
No. Hospitality and tourism education can cover multiple professional areas, including food and beverage, tourism, guest service, operations and professional leadership.

Fees & Scholarships

How much are the school fees?
Fees can vary depending on the program and academic year. Contact the admissions or finance office for the current official fee structure.
Are payment plans available?
Payment arrangements depend on the school's current policies. Students should contact the finance or admissions office for the latest available options.
Does the school provide scholarships?
Scholarship and financial-support opportunities may vary by academic year. Contact the school directly for current information and eligibility requirements.

Careers & Internships

Does the school provide internship opportunities?
Hospitality education commonly includes opportunities for students to gain practical industry experience. Contact the school to learn about current internship arrangements and partners.
What careers can hospitality students pursue?
Graduates can pursue careers across hotels, restaurants, tourism businesses, events, guest relations, food and beverage, travel and other hospitality-related organizations.
Does the school teach professional leadership?
Professional development can include communication, teamwork, service standards, leadership, problem solving and other skills needed in hospitality workplaces.

Student Life

What is student life like?
Student life is designed around learning, practical development, teamwork and professional preparation within a hospitality-focused environment.
Can students participate in activities outside class?
Activities can include student events, professional development activities, hospitality projects and community-focused experiences depending on the academic calendar.
How can students get more information?
Students and prospective applicants can contact the school through the admissions desk or submit an enquiry using the enquiry form on this page.
