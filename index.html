About the Institution

Shaping the future of African hospitality.

The Rwanda School of Hospitality is dedicated to developing the next generation of hospitality professionals through excellence, discipline, practical training, and genuine African warmth.

From the classroom to real-world service environments, our students develop the technical expertise, confidence, character, and leadership skills required to succeed in Rwanda and on the international hospitality stage.
